# Battlecard — BeepBeep AI vs the field

*One-page reference. Last updated 23 July 2026. Verify pricing/features on each plugin page before quoting — they change.*

## BeepBeep in one line
Free, WordPress-native AI alt text generator with a built-in review-and-approve step — nothing goes live until a human checks it. 10 generations before signup, then 50/month free; WooCommerce included.

**Own this sentence:** *"Everyone else auto-writes alt text and hopes. BeepBeep makes you approve it first."*

---

## AltText.ai (the incumbent — ~97k sites)
**Their pitch:** the everywhere alt-text engine, 100 to 100,000 images, from $5/mo.
**Genuinely strong at:** integration breadth (WordPress, Shopify, Magento, BigCommerce, API, browser extensions), 130+ languages, AVIF/SVG, transparent pricing, roll-over credits, brand trust.
**Vulnerable on:** only 25 *one-time* free credits (no ongoing free tier); per-image metering; recent WordPress site-crash reports (v1.10.25/1.10.26, Feb 2026); big/impersonal support.
**Beat them by:** free monthly tier + review gate + no-signup trial. Don't fight on breadth — you'll lose.

## AI Alt Text Generator (migkapa — 1,000+ installs, 4.8★)
**Their pitch:** transparent, at-cost, no lock-in — bring your own OpenAI/Anthropic key, or 50/mo managed. WP-CLI, custom prompts, SEO keyphrase integration.
**Genuinely strong at:** developer story, BYO-key/no-lock-in, WP-CLI, extensibility hooks, free.
**Vulnerable on:** bulk batch "stalls out" (reviewer's words + an unanswered July 2026 bulk bug); WooCommerce is Pro-only; slow support ("0 of 2 resolved in last two months"); needs an API key (friction for non-devs).
**Beat them by:** no key/no setup, WooCommerce free, and the review gate. For non-technical owners, "no API key" wins.

## Alt Magic (1,000+ installs, 5.0★, fast-growing)
**Their pitch:** built for scale — 10,000 images/hour, 150+ languages, AVIF/SVG/HEIC, AI image renaming, credit packs.
**Genuinely strong at:** momentum (18×5★, many June 2026), speed, format + language breadth, renaming as a second hook, fast support.
**Vulnerable on:** cloud-only, needs account + API key; no approve-before-save *gate*; SEO-first, lighter on accessibility rigor.
**Beat them by:** the review gate and accessibility framing. Concede speed; reframe on "fast isn't helpful if the text is wrong and already live."

## Our differentiators (with why-it-matters)
1. **Review-and-approve gate** — no unreviewed AI text hits a live/client site. Matters for agencies + WCAG/EAA compliance.
2. **10 generations, no account** — lowest-friction trial in the category. Matters for cautious buyers.
3. **WooCommerce free** — product/gallery alt included, not gated. Matters vs the closest free competitor.
4. **Managed, zero-setup** — no API key, no external console, all in wp-admin. Matters for non-technical owners.

## Landmines to set (ask prospects early)
- "How do you currently check AI-generated alt text before it goes live on a client's site?"
- "Is your free allowance one-time, or does it refresh each month?"
- "Does your current tool cover WooCommerce product images without a paid upgrade?"

## Where we lose (be honest)
Cross-platform/Shopify users, very high-volume shops needing proven throughput, buyers who require social proof today, and anyone needing AVIF/SVG if we haven't confirmed support. Don't force those — focus on WordPress-native, accessibility-minded, and non-technical owners.
